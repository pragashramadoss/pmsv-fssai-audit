How scoring works

Normal requirements: C = 2, PC = 1 and NC = 0.

Critical (*) requirements: shown with a light grey/beige background and scored out of 4. Only C, NC or NA can be selected; PC is not applicable. C = 4 and NC = 0.

Not Applicable (NA): no marks are awarded and the requirement is removed from the applicable maximum score. Example: one normal 2-point requirement marked NA changes the denominator from 92 to 90; a 4-point critical requirement marked NA changes it from 92 to 88.

Critical rule: A Non-Compliance (NC) in even one critical (*) requirement makes the overall assessment Non Compliance / No Grade, irrespective of the percentage achieved.
